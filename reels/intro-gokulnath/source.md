## Adapted telling (what the reel dramatises)

One day Gokulnathji was telling the varta of the 84 Vaishnavs, sitting with
Kalyanbhatt and the other Vaishnavs. He was so lost in the rasa of the telling
that he forgot to speak the Subodhini katha. Half the night went by.

A Vaishnav asked him: "Maharajadhiraj — when will you tell the katha today?"

It was already midnight. Gokulnathji answered: "Today I am telling you the
*fruit* of the katha. Know that the whole fruit lies in the words of the
Vaishnavs. There is nothing beyond the Vaishnav. This is Pushtimarg — it bears
fruit through the Vaishnav. Acharyaji said the same: 'Damla, I revealed this
path for you.' So know the Vaishnav's varta to be supreme."

## Doctrinal frame (p1–2, not spoken in the reel — context only)

- 84 lakh yonis; divine souls falling into them; 4 kinds of soul: राजसी, तामसी, सात्विकी, निर्गुण.
- Acharyaji manifests 84 Vaishnavs to liberate the निर्गुण souls.
- 84 kos of Braj; 84 varnas of rasa.
- Gusainji's three gunamaya groups number 252 — a different work (दो सौ बावन वैष्णवन की वार्ता), not this reel.
