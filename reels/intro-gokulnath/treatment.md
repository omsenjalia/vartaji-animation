# Treatment — Intro: आधी रात की वार्ता

**Archetype:** The Meeting — the entire reel is one exchange, with the assembly
as witness. No quest, no reversal of fortune, no miracle. The drama is a
question asked at the wrong time and an answer that reframes the whole book.

**Motif object:** the oil lamp. It gutters, burns low, then its light grows as
Gokulnathji speaks. The lamp is the only thing that "acts" besides the people.

**Runtime target:** 88 s (10 shots). Story length decides, not a format.

## The six decisions

1. **Whose eyes?** A young Vaishnav in the back row. We never see his face
   clearly until the last shot; we see what he sees — the back of Gokulnathji's
   head, the lamp, the shadows, then the answer. The audience learns with him.
2. **What shape?** The Meeting. One question, one answer, nothing else happens.
   The doctrinal weight lands in the answer, never in a voiceover explanation.
3. **What's the object?** The oil lamp (अट्टदीप). One flame, carried across all
   ten shots, changing state.
4. **What's withheld, and until when?** Gokulnathji's face and eyes until shot 7.
   Before the answer he is a silhouette / a voice. The first time we see his
   eyes is the first word of the answer.
5. **Where does the camera live?** Ground level, inside the circle. No god-view,
   no overhead. The slow push in shot 8 is the only move that feels like a
   reveal.
6. **How does it end?** Not on a moral card. End on Gokulnathji's hand resting
   on the closed manuscript and the lamp flame in the same frame. The last line
   is spoken over that image, then one beat of silence.

## Visual grammar

- **Night forest clearing.** A large kadamba tree at the back; Gokulnathji sits
  on a raised bench beneath it. A campfire burns in the middle. The Vaishnavs
  sit in a semicircle below, facing the fire and the teacher. Moonlit tree
  silhouettes surround the clearing.
- Opening camera starts high above the clearing, then descends to ground level
  over the first beat (the "pan in from the top" the user asked for).
- Firelight is the key light: warm amber from below, cool moonlight as fill.
  Faces are lit by the fire — intimate, flickering, alive.
- No cut within a beat. Each shot is one held composition with internal motion.
- The 84 are suggested, not counted: a semicircle of 13 figures, the rest lost
  in darkness.
- Devanagari captions: 2–3 words from `captions.json`, timed to narration.

## Sound

- Base: low room tone, distant crickets, a brass lamp wick crackle.
- One recurring sound motif: the wick's flicker, which stops at shot 7 when the
  answer begins.
- No devotional music bed under the narration. A single tanpura drone enters at
  shot 8 and stays under the last two shots.
